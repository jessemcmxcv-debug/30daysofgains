Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 30 Days of Gains (“the App,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and stores information when you use our website and progressive web app.

Short version: We collect account info if you sign in with Google, workout progress you log, and optional Community posts. We don’t sell your personal data.

1. Information we collect

If you continue as Guest

  • Workout progress and settings stored on your device only (browser local storage)
  • No Google account required

If you sign in with Google

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (if provided by Google)
  • Workout progress and achievements, synced to our database so you can use multiple devices

Community features

  • Photos and text you choose to post
  • Display name shown with your posts
  • Likes and comments you submit

Technical data

  • Basic device/browser information needed to run the app
  • Optional local notifications if you enable daily reminders

2. How we use information

  • To provide and improve the App
  • To sync your workout progress across devices when signed in
  • To display Community content you choose to share
  • To operate admin features for managing workout programs

3. Where data is stored

  • On your device: local progress, settings, private progress photos
  • In the cloud (Supabase): Google-linked progress, Community posts/images/comments, shared workout programs
  • Google: handles sign-in; we receive basic profile info via Google Identity Services

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Content you post to Community is visible to other users of the App. Service providers (such as Supabase for hosting data, and Google for authentication) process data only to run the App.

5. Your choices

  • Use Guest mode without creating an account
  • Sign out at any time in Settings
  • Avoid posting photos or personal details in Community if you prefer privacy
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove local storage on that device
  • Request deletion of your cloud progress or Community content by contacting us (see below)

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

Guest data remains on your device until you clear it. Signed-in progress and Community content remain until you request deletion or the App is discontinued. We may remove inactive or abusive content.

8. Security

We use standard industry practices (HTTPS, access controls via our database provider). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the App operator via the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen for this project, or through the contact method provided with the App.
